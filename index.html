About the solution

Vocca: conversational AI voice agents for healthcare centers

Vocca is a dedicated AI voice agent for clinics and medical centers. It answers calls, handles appointment scheduling, sends reminders, and notes patient requests automatically. Our proprietary conversational AI speaks directly with patients on the phone, answers typical questions in natural language, and records relevant details to the clinic’s systems. Patients no longer wait on hold or type through complex menus—the agent is always available, 24/7.

Regional deployment in the EU
Patient calls are processed in real time through Google Cloud AI and speech services; intents such as booking, rescheduling or requesting information are routed to Vocca’s workflow engine and stored in secure databases before syncing with clinic systems.
  • Real-time voice understanding. Vocca uses Google Cloud speech technologies to convert patient speech into text and back into natural voice responses with low latency, even during complex multi-turn conversations.
  • Intent-driven workflows. AI models determine what the caller wants—book an appointment, change a time, ask about opening hours or preparation—and trigger the correct workflow, including reminders and notes.
  • Secure data handling. Appointment and call metadata are stored in managed databases on Google Cloud with strict access control, encryption, and regional data residency aligned with European privacy expectations.
  • Easy integration with clinic systems. Vocca exposes RESTful APIs and webhooks that connect to electronic scheduling or practice-management solutions, keeping staff calendars and reminders in sync.
